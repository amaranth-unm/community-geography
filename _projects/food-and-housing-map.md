This is a map of places to access food and housing resources that are available for UNM students. They were chosen through a comprehensive review of many food and housing resourecs across Albuquerque. The resources are sorted into different categories: on-campus resources, immediate, long-term, for families. The map was created by Ramona Malczynski for the [UNM Basic Needs Project](https://basicneeds.unm.edu/), with support from the R.H. Mallory Center for Community Geography.

View the map [here](https://www.google.com/maps/d/edit?mid=110jzn2v86K65t-6px1Akt4oZx8rVYeI&usp=sharing).
