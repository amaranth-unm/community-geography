Katie Slack is a second year doctoral student in the department of Geography and Environmental Studies. They are interested in using GIS and web mapping to further foster biodiversity and resilient urban environments in the face of climate change. Katie is interested in more-than-human geography, political ecology, and critical physical geography. Outside of academics Katie enjoys spending time with their cats, exploring the outdoors, and reading sci-fi and fantasy novels.
