summary: "Faculty Affiliation Program\r\n\r\nThe Center for Community Geography seeks faculty affiliates willing to support\
  \ its mission in one or more of the following ways:\r\n\r\nIdentify potential partnerships and/or projects in the Albuquerque\
  \ area with a focus in human-environment relations.\r\nReview proposals for community-engaged research (for grad students)\
  \ and/or community-engaged teaching (for faculty).\r\nPropose and/or participate in events that bring university and community\
  \ knowledge producers together.\r\nDevelop strategies to value local environmental knowledge in academic research.\r\nParticipate\
  \ in an annual meeting to discuss broad initiatives in community engagement at UNM.\r\n\r\nFaculty affiliation will convey\
  \ the following rights and responsibilities:\r\nAffiliate profiles will be listed (and annually updated) on the Center for\
  \ Community Geography website.\r\nAffiliates will be added to the Center's listserv for notification of events and initiatives.\r\
  \nAffiliates will be eligible to submit grants through the Center, which provides F&A both to the PI and to the PI's department.\r\
  \n\r\nAffiliates will be eligible for administrative support for projects and events that support the Center's mission.\r\
  \nAffiliates will be invited annually to provide feedback on Center strategy and initiatives.\r\n\r\nEligibility\r\nAny\
  \ UNM faculty member or postdoc is eligible to apply for affiliation, regardless of discipline or institutional location,\
  \ if their interests align broadly with the Center's mission."
