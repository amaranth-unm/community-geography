# Donate to the Center for Community Geography

The R.H. Mallory Center for Community Geography builds and maintains partnerships between UNM and New Mexico’s communities to effectively address human-environment issues. The center is entirely funded by donations. [Your contributions](https://www.unmfund.org/fund/r-h-mallory/) help us support students and faculty in community-engaged teaching, research and learning.
