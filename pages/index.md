The R.H. Mallory Center for Community Geography connects UNM resources with community needs through collaborative research, learning, and action.
{: .welcome-lede}

Our work brings together service learning, collaborative mapping, place-based research, and public events that strengthen relationships between the university and communities across New Mexico.

We welcome students, faculty, and community partners who want to address critical human-environment issues together.
