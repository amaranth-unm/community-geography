# Funding opportunities

**Funding for UNM [students]({{ site.baseurl }}/funding/grad-fellowships) and [faculty]({{ site.baseurl }}/funding/seed-funding):** We fund community-engaged work through two annual funding competitions (info on [fellowships]({{ site.baseurl }}/funding/grad-fellowships) and [classroom fund](https://canvas.unm.edu/courses/39553/pages) pages). In both competitions, priority is given to applicants committed to principles of community engagement that value local knowledge and support community-led work. 

**Funding for community partners:** We also directly fund work with community partners, primarily through external funding. If you have a project idea and would like to discuss the potential for working with us to find external funding through a university-community partnership, [contact us]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/contact-us)!
