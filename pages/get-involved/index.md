# Get involved

The Center puts UNM students and faculty to work on human-environment questions that matter to New Mexico communities. There are a few ways in, depending on where you sit.

## Partner with us

If you represent a group, community, agency, school, or place in New Mexico that could benefit from the involvement of UNM students or researchers, we want to hear from you. [Find out how partnerships work]({{ site.baseurl }}/get-involved/partner), or [contact us]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/contact-us) with a project idea. We also fund work with community partners directly, primarily through external funding, and can help find that funding through a university-community partnership.

## Funding for UNM students and faculty

We fund community-engaged work through two annual competitions. In both, priority is given to applicants committed to principles of community engagement that value local knowledge and support community-led work.

[**Community Geography Fellows Program (for students)**]({{ site.baseurl }}/get-involved/fellowships)

[**Classroom Fund for Community Engagement (for faculty)**]({{ site.baseurl }}/get-involved/classroom-fund)

## Donate

The Center is entirely funded by donations. [Give to the Center]({{ site.baseurl }}/get-involved/donate) to support students and faculty in community-engaged teaching, research, and learning.
