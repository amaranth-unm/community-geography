# R.H. Mallory Center for Community Geography Classroom Fund for Community Engagement

The R.H. Center for Community Geography accepts applications each year for the Classroom Fund for Community Engagement. Grants of up to \$1,000 will be considered, and a total of \$1,500 will be awarded. The objective of these grants is to provide seed funding that helps faculty members engage their students directly with community-led projects that enhance students' learning experiences while also contributing to community goals and projects centered on human-environment issues. Priority is given to applicants who clearly articulate and demonstrate how their project or class is centered on [principles of community engagement]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/community-geography) that value local knowledge and support community-led work to achieve positive community change. For examples of pilot projects currently underway, visit our [Projects Page.]({{ site.baseurl }}/projects/)

For more information, contact the Center at <communitygeography@unm.edu>.
