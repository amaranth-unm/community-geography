--- layout: base-unm --- {% comment %} Lexicon word pages (_lexicon/). The page is built from the word's front matter, top to bottom: - word, definition: the heading and the sentence or two under it - definition_source: (optional) who the definition is quoted from - image, image_alt, image_caption: a wide photo across the window - terms_heading, terms: related ideas in a gold band (heading default: " engages…") - story: the rest of the page, in order. Each item is one of: a quote text and source; shown large, with a quotation mark a passage text (Markdown: paragraphs, lists, links), with an optional heading and credit (a line below it, shown as written, such as "Source: …" or "Credit: …") a photo photo only; shown across the text column A quote or passage can also have a photo beside it (photo, photo_alt, photo_caption); those photos alternate sides, and on a wide screen a photo beside long text stays in view while the text scrolls. `poem: true` keeps a quote's line breaks (a blank line starts a new stanza). Any text below the front matter comes last, such as references. {% endcomment %} {% assign body = content | strip %}
