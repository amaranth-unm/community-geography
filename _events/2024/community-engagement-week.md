#  

**Save the Date: April 1- 5, 2024: UNM Community Engagement Week**  
  
Join us at the heart of campus, the SUB, as we unite for a transformative experience. This year's Community Engagement Week is not just a series of events—it's a celebration of collaboration, service, and the power of community.

**[Click here for a detailed schedule of events!](http://news.unm.edu/news/unm-hosts-community-engagement-week-2024)**

### [**Office of Community Engagement**](https://engagement.unm.edu/)

**April 1 & 2, 2024**

**Elevating Community Engagement at UNM**

The Office of Community Engagement is organizing a series of workshops aimed at exploring ways to enhance support for community-engaged scholarship in higher education.

[Register](https://forms.office.com/r/XkVyCwxXXp) for the panels (Deadline: March 22, 2024).

### [**Center for Teaching and Learning**](https://ctl.unm.edu/)

[2024 Spring Teaching Conference](https://ctl.unm.edu/instructors/spring-teaching-conference/spring-teaching-conference-2024.html)

**April 3 & 4, 2024**

Community-Engaged Teaching & Learning

This conference aims to create a space where instructors across UNM and community partners can learn from each other and inspire impactful and inclusive community-engaged teaching and learning.

Learn more, and [register ](https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=MJiqJfngK0iJfho7PIVeXBaKkgyztRhNrBfQwH9k7fNUMTNQR1pWNUhUV1ZUUTNES0E0N0lHTUpHNyQlQCN0PWcu)for the conference [](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=MJiqJfngK0iJfho7PIVeXBaKkgyztRhNrBfQwH9k7fNUREpaQUQzMTJURzRIWEFHMjI2N1dUUE9HOCQlQCN0PWcu)(Deadline: March 22, 2024)

### [**Community Engagement Center**](https://communityengagement.unm.edu/)

**April 5, 2024**

The UNM Community Engagement Center invites you to join us for the 14th Annual Community Engagement Center Symposium. This year we want to reflect on OUR-Community: Organizing for change, Unveiling voices, Re-kindling relations —in Community. 

[Register](https://forms.gle/RjoHV1JNg31snVYm6) for the workshops (Deadline: March 22, 2024)
